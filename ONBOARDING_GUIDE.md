## Finding Your First Arbitrage Opportunity (10 minutes)

### Understanding Arbitrage

An **arbitrage opportunity** (or "surebet") is a guaranteed profit situation where you can place bets on all outcomes of a match and win regardless of the result.

**Example**:
- Bookmaker A: Home wins at 2.5 odds
- Bookmaker B: Away wins at 2.5 odds
- If you bet $100 on Home and $100 on Away, you guarantee a profit

### How to Spot Opportunities

1. **Go to Dashboard**: Click the home icon in the sidebar
2. **Look at Opportunities**: Scroll to the "Active Opportunities" section
3. **Check the Profit %**: Higher percentage = better opportunity
4. **Review Risk Level**: Low risk is safer than high risk

### Key Metrics Explained

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Profit %** | Guaranteed profit as percentage of total stake |
| **ROI** | Return on investment (profit ÷ stake × 100) |
| **Risk Level** | Low/Medium/High based on odds reliability |
| **Type** | 2-way (Home/Away) or 3-way (Home/Draw/Away) |

## Placing Your First Bet (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Select an Opportunity

1. Find an opportunity with:
   - Profit % > 2%
   - Risk Level: Low
   - Type: 2-way (easier to understand)

2. Click on the opportunity to view details

### Step 2: Review the Details

You'll see:
- **Match**: Team names and time
- **Bookmakers**: Where to place each bet
- **Odds**: The exact odds for each outcome
- **Stake Distribution**: How much to bet on each outcome
- **Guaranteed Profit**: Your profit if you follow the stakes

### Step 3: Place the Bets

1. Note the stake for each outcome
2. Go to each bookmaker's website
3. Place the bets exactly as shown
4. Return to ArbitrageHub and mark as placed

### Step 4: Track Your Bet

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Betting History**
2. Your bet appears with:
   - Status (Pending/Won/Lost)
   - Profit/Loss amount
   - ROI percentage

## Common Questions

### Q: Is arbitrage betting legal?

**A**: Yes, arbitrage betting is completely legal. You're simply taking advantage of different odds across bookmakers. Bookmakers don't like it, but they can't stop you.

### Q: Can I lose money?

**A**: True arbitrage opportunities guarantee profit. However, if you don't follow the exact stake distribution, you could lose money. Always double-check the stakes before placing bets.

### Q: How much can I make?

**A**: Typical arbitrage profit is 1-5% per bet. If you place 10 bets per day at 3% profit on $100 stakes, you'd make $30/day or $900/month.

### Q: Why do I need multiple bookmakers?

**A**: Different bookmakers have different odds. Arbitrage opportunities exist because of these differences. More bookmakers = more opportunities.

### Q: What's the difference between 2-way and 3-way arbitrage?

**A**: 2-way arbitrage covers Home/Away outcomes. 3-way arbitrage includes Draw. 3-way opportunities are rarer but can have higher profit.

### Q: Can I use bots to place bets?

**A**: Most bookmakers prohibit automated betting. We recommend placing bets manually. Premium tier includes API access for advanced users.

### Q: What if a bet doesn't settle as expected?

**A**: Contact the bookmaker's support. ArbitrageHub is not responsible for bet settlement. Always read the bookmaker's terms.
